the mission

built to help you think, rest, and breathe

pure flow exists for one reason: to help you think, rest, and breathe.

it's not a subscription or a platform. there is no account or cloud, no analytics, advertising, and no data leaving your device.

pure flow is free to use for the essentials. if you choose to upgrade, it's a single, one-time purchase. you own it forever. every sound, every update, every feature added. no subscriptions. no recurring fees. no gotchas.

have a problem? tell me. want a sound added? let me know.

this app is built by one person who needed something like this and couldn't find it. if it helps you the way it helps me, that's everything.

built offline. stays offline. always.

what is pure flow

sound that works as hard as you do

∞
gapless looping
Dual-instance crossfade means you never hear the seam. The next loop is already playing before the current one ends. Seamlessly, every time, forever.
✈
fully offline
Zero network permissions. Works in airplane mode. No tracking.
◎
layered sfx
Stack ambient drones with intermittent effects. Control density and volume per layer.
◷
sleep timer
Auto-fades before shutting off so there's no audio shock.
♡
saved flows
Save your perfect sound combinations and restore them instantly.
☽
background audio
Plays reliably through your lock screen with full media controls.
